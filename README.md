# Barclays Corporate Banking

Discover the new Barclays Corporate Banking experience—bringing together business banking solutions, industry expertise and insights to help your business move forward.

## Open the website

Extract ZIP file. Then open **index.html** in a web browser.

## What you’ll find on the page

- **Navigation** to help you move around the page
- **Welcome banner** introducing Barclays Corporate Banking
- **Banking solutions** for businesses
- **Why Barclays** and the bank’s approach
- **Industry expertise**
- **Business insights**
- **Contact and enquiry form**
- **Footer** with useful links

The page adapts to desktop, tablet, and mobile screen sizes.

## How it is built

The page uses semantic HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. It does not require third-party libraries or frameworks.

## About the enquiry form

The form area is organised into two tabs: **Enquiry form** and **Loan calculator**. Select a tab to switch between them. You can also use the Left and Right arrow keys to move between tabs, Home and End to jump to the first or last tab, and Tab to move into the selected panel.

The enquiry form checks required details, email formats, and privacy consent. The separate loan calculator updates an illustrative monthly repayment as you enter a loan amount, annual rate, and term. The estimate assumes fixed monthly repayments and excludes fees; it is not a Barclays offer or lending decision.

Choose **Sole Trader** or **Limited Company** to reveal the relevant business details. The optional authorised signatories area lets you add and remove as many signatory groups as needed. Any added group requires a name, role, and valid email address.

After a valid entry, the form displays a demo confirmation in the browser. **Thank you for your interest in Barclays Corporate Banking. Your information has been submitted successfully.**
